Day 20 — 5 Words with Hindi & English Meanings

Mellifluous

adjective
HINDI MEANING
मधुर / सुरीला / मीठा
*Madhur / Sureela / Meetha*
ENGLISH MEANING
Sweet or musical; pleasant to hear.
  • The singer captivated the audience with her mellifluous voice.
  • He fell asleep listening to the mellifluous sound of the rain.
  • The flute played a mellifluous tune that echoed through the valley.
ALTERNATE WORDS:
dulcet melodious musical sweet-sounding harmonious

Alacrity

noun
HINDI MEANING
तत्परता / उत्सुकता / तेजी
*Tatparta / Utsukta / Teji*
ENGLISH MEANING
Brisk and cheerful readiness to do something.
  • She accepted the job offer with alacrity.
  • The eager students responded to the teacher's question with alacrity.
  • He moved with surprising alacrity for a man of his age.
ALTERNATE WORDS:
eagerness readiness enthusiasm promptness zeal

Obfuscate

verb
HINDI MEANING
अस्पष्ट करना / भ्रमित करना / उलझाना
*Aspasht karna / Bhramit karna / Uljhaana*
ENGLISH MEANING
To deliberately make something unclear or difficult to understand.
  • The politician tried to obfuscate the issue with vague answers.
  • Do not write complex code that will only obfuscate the main logic.
  • Her accusation was designed to obfuscate the truth of the matter.
ALTERNATE WORDS:
confuse complicate blur muddle cloud

Capricious

adjective
HINDI MEANING
मनमौजी / अस्थिर / सनकी
*Manmauji / Asthir / Sanki*
ENGLISH MEANING
Given to sudden and unaccountable changes of mood or behavior.
  • The administration of justice shouldn't be capricious or arbitrary.
  • Her capricious behavior made it difficult to maintain a stable relationship.
  • We cannot rely on the capricious weather of this region for our outdoor event.
ALTERNATE WORDS:
fickle unpredictable volatile erratic whimsical

Quintessential

adjective
HINDI MEANING
सर्वोत्कृष्ट / आदर्श / मूलतत्वीय
*Sarvotkrisht / Aadarsh / Mooltatviya*
ENGLISH MEANING
Representing the most perfect or typical example of a quality or class.
  • Watermelon is the quintessential fruit of summer.
  • He was the quintessential gentleman, always polite and helpful.
  • This village is the quintessential British countryside destination.
ALTERNATE WORDS:
typical classic exemplary ultimate archetypal
